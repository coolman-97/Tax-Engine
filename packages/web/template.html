Deterministic real-estate tax engine

Every number here has
someone behind it.

An advisor is about to tell a client what to do with a third of their net worth, and they will defend the number out loud with their license behind it. So this engine treats where a number came from as a property of the number. When an input is not attested, it does not return a figure with an asterisk — it refuses, and hands back the shortest list of questions that would unblock it, priced.

Figures are computed by the Python engine in packages/engine and exported as a scenario bundle; this page renders that bundle and never does tax arithmetic of its own. Every amount is carried as integer cents. The household and its documents are synthetic.

Every figure traces back to a document page, a named attestation, or a stated assumption. A derived number is exactly as defensible as its weakest input.
